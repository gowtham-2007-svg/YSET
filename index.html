Terms & Conditions

1. General Portal Usage

This portal is exclusively intended for bona fide enrolled students and scholars of Yenepoya (Deemed to be University) for official student queries, academic service requests, and grievance registration.

2. Accuracy of Information

All information provided (such as Campus ID, Registered Email, and Phone Number) must match university records. Submission of fraudulent information or queries under impersonation is strictly prohibited under university regulations.

3. Processing Timelines

Standard queries will be acknowledged within 24 business hours. Complex academic inquiries or certificate issuances are subject to official university departmental turnaround schedules.

4. Privacy & Data Protection

All personal identification details collected via this portal are strictly secured and used solely for university administrative communication and request resolution.
